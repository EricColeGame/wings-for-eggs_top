## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does the wings for eggs limited wings quest stay available?**
It runs for a fixed event window shown on the in-game timer. When that timer ends, the exchange closes, so treat the final days as a hard deadline rather than assuming an extension is coming.

Usually not. Most events reset egg inventories between rotations. Spend what you earn inside the current window instead of hoarding for a future one.

Yes. Focus on the highest-value egg sources, skip low-tier exchanges, and bank everything for a single top-tier unlock. Late starters who plan well often finish with the best wing in the lobby.
