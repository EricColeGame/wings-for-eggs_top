## The Core Loop: How Eggs Turn Into Wings

Every egg-to-wing economy runs on three steps: earn eggs, hatch or hold them, then trade the results for cosmetics. Eggs are easy to obtain early and much harder to obtain later, which is exactly why they work as currency. That distinction matters at the negotiating table. Four things drive the loop:

- **Scarcity.** Eggs retired after an event become progressively harder to find.
- **Hatch odds.** The rarer the top hatch, the more the sealed egg is worth.
- **Cosmetic demand.** Wings are pure status, so trends move fast and can reverse overnight.
- **Tradability.** If an item cannot be traded, it cannot be part of a deal, no matter how rare it looks.

Player experience suggests that the most consistent traders treat eggs like a savings account. They hold sealed eggs from limited events, skip hype purchases, and only spend when a wing they actually want appears in a trade window.

### Building an Offer That Gets Accepted

- Lead with the item you care about least, not the one you care about most.
- Bundle two mid-tier eggs rather than one high-tier egg when possible.
- Mention what makes your egg special — event name, release window, or rarity tier.
- Be willing to add a small filler item to close a gap.
- Revisit a stalled trade later; sellers often soften after the initial hype fades.
