## Which Wings Are Worth Trading — and Which to Hold

Not all wings deserve the same treatment. The general rule is that effort-earned wings hold more exchange value than anything you can buy, and duplicates are always the safest thing to trade away.

| Wing tier | Typical source | Relative exchange value | Best move |
|---|---|---|---|

| Rare boss or raid wings | Group content and boss drops | High | Hold for a bonus window if one is active |
| Limited seasonal wings | Time-gated events | Highest | Hold unless you own a duplicate |
| Premium store wings | Direct purchase or gifting | Usually ineligible | Do not trade — check the tag first |

The logic behind "duplicates first" is simple: a wing you own twice has no collection value, while a wing you own once may never return if the event that granted it never runs again. Seasonal cosmetics are the clearest example — they are the items players most often regret trading.

If you are unsure whether a wing is safe to convert, ask yourself one question: could I earn this again in the next month? If the answer is no, hold it.
