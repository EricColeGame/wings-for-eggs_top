- Egg demand spikes at launch, then settles as supply catches up.
- Trade volume typically peaks in the first few days, not weeks.
- Casual players benefit most from simple, low-risk, one-for-one exchanges.

## Valuing Wings vs. Eggs: A Practical Framework

Value in these systems is never a single number. It is a blend of scarcity, demand, and how easy the item is to move. Use the table below to score both sides of a trade before you commit.

| Factor | What to look for | Weight in a trade |
|---|---|---|
| Rarity | Limited supply, retired sources, event-only drops | High |
| Demand | How many players actively want it right now | High |
| Hatch odds | Chance of getting something valuable from the egg | Medium to high |
| Exclusivity | Whether the item can return in a future event | Medium |
| Liquidity | How quickly you can trade it again if needed | Medium |
| Aesthetics | Personal preference and community taste | Low to medium |

A practical rule: never trade a scarce, retired wing for a currently farmable egg unless the egg has confirmed high-value hatch odds. Scarcity is hard to replace. Supply is not.

| Trade scenario | Likely verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Retired wing for common egg | Avoid | You give up the scarcer item |
| Common wing for event egg | Usually good | Event eggs often hold value after the window closes |
| Any wing for an egg you will hatch immediately | Depends | Hatch odds matter more than the egg itself |
| Duplicate wing for a missing egg | Usually good | You convert surplus into collection progress |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Better approach |
|---|---|---|
| Trading without checking eligibility | You may convert the wrong item | Verify the eligible list first |
| Assuming the wing comes back | Some conversions are permanent | Confirm before confirming |
| Using unofficial middlemen | High risk of losing items entirely | Stick to official in-game menus |
| Chasing launch-day hype | Prices are at their least reliable | Wait for the first correction |
| Ignoring hatch odds | A rare egg can still hatch something common | Check published odds if available |
| Converting everything at once | Leaves you with no flexibility | Convert in small batches |

The middleman point is not optional. Any exchange that requires handing your items to another player outside the official system is a risk you do not need to take, especially when an in-game menu already does the same job.

## Community Reports vs. Official Patch Notes

Player experience is useful, but it is not the same as confirmed information. Keep the two separate:

- **Official notes** confirm mechanics: which wings qualify, whether items are consumed, and how long the window lasts.
- **Community reports** describe outcomes: what people actually received, how long queues were, and which trades felt fair.
- **Player experience** varies wildly by server, region, and time of day, so treat single anecdotes as data points, not rules.
- **Cross-check anything surprising.** If a claim sounds too generous, look for a second and third source before acting on it.

When official notes and community reports disagree, trust the official notes for mechanics and the community for sentiment. Mechanics are documented; feelings are observed.
